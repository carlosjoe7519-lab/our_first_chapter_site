My Princess, My Lovee.

If you've made it all the way here, I hope you've felt even a little of what I wanted to give you through this little website: a reminder that you are loved, thought about, appreciated, and worth making an effort for.

These two months have given me memories I want to keep close. I think about how we started, how we slowly became comfortable with each other, and how one day became the beginning of us. I know we're still learning. We won't get every moment right, and there will be days when things feel harder than others. But I don't want us to forget to be kind to each other, to communicate, to laugh, and to keep choosing one another.

Thank you for the little things you do, for letting me into your world, for the conversations, the videos, the laughter, and for being part of this chapter of my life. I hope I make you feel as special as you are to me.

I made this because I wanted to give you something you could come back to — something that says, even on a normal day, “Hey, someone loves you and is grateful you're in their life.”

Happy two months, my princess. This is only our second chapter. I can't wait to see what we write next, together.

Forever yours,
Carlos ❤️
