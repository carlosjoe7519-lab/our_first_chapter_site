Chapter five · For every kind of day

Letters For You

Three little letters for the days you need a reminder of how much you matter to me.

Read this on an ordinary day

My favourite person,

I hope you know that you don't have to do anything extraordinary to make me happy. I love the ordinary parts of having you in my life: hearing about your day, seeing your little videos, laughing at something silly, and knowing that somewhere in the middle of your busy day, we get to share a moment. You don't have to be perfect around me. You can be tired, quiet, excited, confused, or completely random. I want to keep getting to know every version of you. Thank you for being you, my lovee.

With love, Carlos ♡

Read this when you doubt yourself

My beautiful girl,

On the days when you don't feel your best, I hope you remember that one difficult day doesn't change how special you are. You are allowed to have off days. You are allowed to rest, to take your time, and to figure things out one step at a time. I hope you speak to yourself with the same kindness you give to the people you care about. I see so many lovely things in you, including the things you might not always notice in yourself. Please don't forget that you are appreciated, you are enough, and you deserve tenderness too.

I'm always cheering for you, Carlos ♡

Read this when you miss me

My lovee,

If I could, I'd close the distance for a little while and come sit beside you. I'd ask about your day, listen to all the little details, and probably find a way to make you laugh. Since I can't always do that right now, let this letter be a small reminder that you're on my mind. I miss the simple things we could do if we were in the same place, and I look forward to making more memories with you. Until then, let's keep sharing our days, making time for each other, and remembering that we're on the same team.

Sending you the biggest hug, Carlos ♡
