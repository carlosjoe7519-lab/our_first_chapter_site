Chapter one · Our beginning

How It All Started

Come here, my lovee. Let me take you back to the beginning for a little while. ❤️

My lovee, sometimes I sit and think about how we even got here, and honestly, it's crazy to think that everything started with something as simple as a TikTok follow.

I remember coming across your profile and seeing you for the first time. You were so beautiful, and I just couldn't bring myself to scroll past you like I hadn't seen something special. So I followed you, hoping maybe you'd notice me. And eventually, you followed me back. Little did I know that one follow would become the beginning of something so important to me.

Now, let me remind you how difficult you were when we first started talking. 😂❤️ My goodness, girl! I could ask you the simplest question, and getting an answer from you felt like a whole mission. You really used to make me mad sometimes. I would wonder why you were making things so difficult for me. But as time went by, something changed. You slowly started trusting me, our conversations became more natural, and we began getting comfortable with each other.

Then came the day I finally got to meet you in person.

Baby, I don't think you fully understand how happy I was that day. Imagine finally getting to see the girl who had caught your attention, not through a screen, not through pictures, but right there in front of you. I was finally meeting my crush! And even though you had your friend with you, I remember feeling like I had your attention, and that made me so happy. It gave me a little hope that maybe this thing between us could actually become something real.

And then we met again.

I still remember our Chicken Inn date. You bought me lunch, and I remember wanting to show you that I could also do little things for you. I started shredding your chicken into strips because I knew you weren't a fan of spicy food. 😂❤️

I also remember how I was running late that day because I was supposed to be cooking at home. But even with everything I had going on, I didn't want to rush the time I was spending with you. I took you to your dad's workplace, and then I headed home.

Looking back, those little moments mean so much to me.

Somewhere along the way, talking to you stopped feeling like trying to impress someone and started feeling like talking to my favourite person. I started receiving those little outfit videos every morning. You would text me as soon as you woke up, and sometimes that meant receiving a message from you at 3 AM! 😂

I loved those little things, baby. The way you started sharing your mornings with me, the random conversations, the videos, and how naturally we were becoming part of each other's everyday lives. I could feel us getting closer, and I loved watching it happen.

And then, my lovee, we got to September 22, 2026.

I know you weren't feeling okay that day. It might not have been the easiest day for you, and I know things weren't exactly perfect. But I want you to remember how that day ended.

We shared our first kiss.

And baby, that moment became the beginning of us. The day all those conversations, little moments, hopes and feelings finally became something we could call ours.

I don't know if you remember that moment the same way I do, but I hope you know how much it means to me. Even though your day hadn't started out well, it gave me one of the happiest memories of my life.

And that's how we got here, princess.

From a TikTok follow, to two strangers learning about each other, to our first meeting, our little Chicken Inn date, those morning outfit videos, the 3 AM messages, and finally becoming each other's person.

Sometimes I wish I could take you back to those first days just so we could experience them again. But then I remember that we aren't still at the beginning. We've already made it through two months, and we have so many more memories waiting for us.

I just wanted to remind you where we started, because I never want us to forget how something so simple became something so special.

You were once the beautiful girl I couldn't scroll past. Now you're the girl I don't want to imagine my days without.

Happy two months, my princess. I love you, my lovee.

Forever yours,
Carlos ❤️
