Chapter four · No matter the miles

Even From Afar

For the days when I wish I could be right there beside you.

My lovee, I know there are days when I wish the distance between us wasn't there. I wish I could just show up, see your face, hold your hand, or give you a hug without having to plan around miles and time.

Sometimes all I want is to be close enough to notice the little things: how your day is going, whether you're tired, what made you smile, or whether you just need someone beside you. A call or a message helps, but I won't pretend it's exactly the same as having you here.

Still, I want you to know that the distance doesn't make you any less important to me. You're still the person I want to tell about my day. You're still the person whose messages can make an ordinary moment feel special. And you're still the girl I think about when I imagine the good things ahead.

We may be in different places right now, but we're still sharing this relationship, learning each other, making memories, and choosing to show up. I don't expect every day to be easy. I just hope we keep being patient with each other, honest with each other, and gentle with each other's hearts.

When you miss me, remember that I'm missing you too. When a day feels long, remember that a difficult day doesn't erase what we're building. And when we finally get to be together again, I hope we appreciate even the smallest things — a walk, a meal, sitting quietly, or simply being in the same place.

The miles may separate where we are, but they don't get to decide how much you mean to me.

I can't promise that I'll get everything right, my princess. But I can promise to keep learning how to love you better, to listen, to communicate, and to make room for you in my life.

Until the next hug, the next date, and the next time I get to look at you in person, please remember that you're loved. Always yours,

Carlos ❤️
